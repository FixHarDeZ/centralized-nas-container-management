# Product Clips are a mode of shorts-factory, not a stack of their own

The Shopee Affiliate account has no Open API access (2026-10-12: the
`/open_api` page shows `AppID: --` and "คุณไม่ได้รับสิทธิ์ในการเข้าถึง Shopee
Affiliate Open API Platform"), so the `shopee-short` plan
(`shopee-short/docs/PLAN.md`) falls back to its manual path: the human picks a
product in the Shopee app, and the bot turns it into a clip the human posts.
Before building a new stack nobody knows will sell anything, the question is
where that clip gets made.

## Decision

`/shop` in shorts-factory. The human sends a **Product Brief** — photos from
the Shopee app plus the share text (name, price, affiliate link, anything they
copy from the listing) — then taps ✅. mimo writes a **Pitch**, the human
reviews it the same way as a Script (type feedback to rewrite, 🎬 to render,
🗑 to drop), and the bot renders a **Product Clip** and sends it back with a
ready-to-paste caption. Posting is done by hand, on whichever platform the
plan settles on. There is no upload button.

- **Reuse, not a copy.** Narration (one synthesis call, sentence boundaries,
  `tighten`), Ken Burns, BGM ducking, the Thai font and Raqm all already work
  here. A separate stack would mean lifting `render.py` into `shared/` first,
  and `render.py` imports `app.footage` and `app.locales`.
- **Its own layout.** The product photo is the picture — no Pexels, no scrim.
  The photo (blurred copy behind it) moves under a text layer that does not:
  badge on top, burned-in subtitle at the bottom, an end card with the price.
  Subtitles are burned in because Shopee Video and Facebook take no `.srt`.
- **Female voice** (`th-TH-PremwadeeNeural`, `SHOP_TTS_VOICE`), so the Product
  Clips do not sound like the explainer channel.
- **The subtitle is what the voice says.** Thai has no spaces to wrap on, so
  the model splits each Beat's narration into at most two on-screen lines, and
  `validate()` sends back a Pitch whose lines do not join to the narration.
- **Numbers only from the Brief, every digit.** Same rule as ADR 0014 but
  stricter: single digits are claims here ("4.9 ดาว", "ลด 5%"). Percentages
  are not written by the model at all — a discount is computed from the two
  prices, so a Pitch cannot quote a number the listing never had.
- **Kept out of the Shorts record.** A Product Clip opens no Manifest, never
  enters `history`, the Experiment or the ADR 0004 Gate, and has no page on
  the dashboard (`/now` shows the raw state, so an open Brief appears there as
  `mode: shop`, link included). Its record is `shop.json` next to the files in
  `/volume1/shorts/shop/<id>/`, and `backfill` skips that folder.
- **One thing at a time.** A Brief can only start from an idle bot, and while
  one is open the bot treats Topics as busy and the unattended trends round
  waits. Same rule the Parked Clip follows for the same reason: one human, one
  phone.

## Consequences

- Reverses the line in `shopee-short/docs/PLAN.md` that said not to use
  shorts-factory for the experiment. That line was about the Topic pipeline
  (upload button to the Thai channel, Tavily facts rejecting prices, Manifests
  mixing into the Experiment); each is answered above.
- If the experiment earns, the Product Clip may still move to its own stack.
  The seam is `app/shop.py` + `app/shop_render.py`; `main.py` only routes.
- No Sub ID is generated by the bot: links from the Shopee app carry none. The
  caption suggests one (the Product Clip id) for a link made by hand in the
  web dashboard.
