**A DETACHED `input[type=file]` silently no-ops `DOM.setFileInputFiles`** — CDP replies `{}`, which looks like success, but the files never reach the app and nothing appears in Uploads. Passing the node's `objectId` instead of `nodeId` does NOT rescue it, and neither does re-appending the detached element to `document.body`: its Angular change listener no longer tracks it.
